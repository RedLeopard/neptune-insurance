01 / THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

A better way through the complicated moments.

Neptune is a fictional regional property-and-casualty insurer migrating from a legacy contact center. The challenge: give policyholders a clear next step without losing their context when they switch channels or teams.

EXPERIENCE 01

Guided FNOL

An interactive, step-by-step simulated first notice of loss captures bounded sample information, creates a browser-local reference, and demonstrates how a claims handoff can be structured.

EXPERIENCE 02

Policyholder workspace

A synthetic customer dashboard surfaces example auto and home coverage, claim references and status timelines. No authentication or real insurance database is represented.

EXPERIENCE 03

Connected support

Support discovery, scripted assistant preview, and precise placeholders for Connect voice, hosted web chat, SMS and email. Channels are only marked live after configuration and verification.

ARCHITECTURE 04

Intent-aware routing

Designs for policy servicing, billing, auto claims, claim status, escalations, and after-hours tasks with queue and routing-profile boundaries.

ARCHITECTURE 05

Guarded Agentic AI

Proposed conversation automation gathers structured intent and summarizes interactions, while authorized business APIs own validation and sensitive decisions.

ARCHITECTURE 06

Observability by design

A traceable contact journey can correlate ContactIds, flow events, contact records, tasks, and secure, minimal customer-context attributes.

02 / THE FLAGSHIP CUSTOMER JOURNEY

One claim. One continuous story.

A realistic demo scenario starts online and illustrates the future end-to-end journey across channels and the specialist team.

05 / ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS

Built like a real system.
Documented like one, too.

The goal is to practice how an architect makes tradeoffs, not just which buttons to click in the console. This is the deployed foundation and the design logic behind it.

Decision 01 / Edge delivery

CloudFront in front of private S3

The website is stored in a dedicated, private S3 bucket and served through a CloudFront distribution. Origin Access Control keeps the bucket from being used as a public website endpoint.

Decision 02 / Identity

Separate domain, shared portfolio

The live customer demo runs at neptune.hireedwardthornton.click. The portfolio case study lives on the main domain, preserving the existing project presentation.

Decision 03 / HTTPS

ACM and Route 53

Route 53 A and AAAA alias records point to CloudFront. An ACM certificate in us-east-1 secures the custom hostname. DNS validation and HTTPS response were verified during deployment.

Decision 04 / CX boundary

Demo interactions before production APIs

Claims and sample policy journeys operate as a client-side prototype. Future real processing will require authenticated APIs, validation, durable storage, and auditable events.

Decision 05 / Capacity

Design for 500, test small

The 500-agent model is a reference architecture, not 500 provisioned Connect users or 500 concurrent calls. Routing profiles, concurrency, and quotas will be modeled separately.

Decision 06 / Messaging

Consent is a real boundary

SMS will be added only after sender verification, compliant opt-in, and successful delivery tests. A fictional insurance demo is not presented as a licensed insurer.
