THE ARCHITECTURE BRIEF

Make the next
step feel clear.

Neptune is a fictional property-and-casualty insurer replacing a legacy contact center. The design challenge is familiar: help a customer reach the right place, and preserve useful context along the way.

The build starts small enough to inspect. The reference architecture looks ahead to a 500-agent operation, while the lab uses a small group of training agents.

CLEAR ROUTING

Capture intent before the handoff.

Menu choices set explicit service context. The claims branch checks that value before selecting and transferring to Auto Claims.

DELIBERATE RECOVERY

Every failure needs a destination.

One retry bounds invalid input and silence. Closed hours, technical failures, and queue capacity each have their own outcome.

HONEST BOUNDARIES

Build the foundation. Prove each layer.

The website, voice entry, routing configuration, and conversational app exist today. AI-to-human handoff, cross-channel context, hosted chat, messaging, and business APIs remain later work.
