NEPTUNETHE EXPERIENCE IS THE PRODUCT
01 / OUR THINKING

A simple belief: support should connect.

Even as a fictional insurer, Neptune is built around real design principles.

Conversations with purpose

Intent first. Less repeating yourself. Every handoff should carry the context needed for the next step.

Privacy by design

Minimize collection, enforce authorization at business-service boundaries, and use synthetic records in the lab.

Clarity in every outcome

Design success, failure, escalation, and after-hours paths instead of creating invisible dead ends.

02 / BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE

One customer journey. Many connected systems.

Our target architecture supports policy service, billing, auto claims, claim status, supervisor escalation, and after-hours follow-up across channels.

The full design explores a 300–500 agent organization while the hands-on lab runs with a few test users. Scale is a design target, not a tested production claim.
