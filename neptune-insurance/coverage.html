03 / BUILDING BLOCKS

Understanding your coverage.

These are common concepts to discuss with a licensed professional at an actual insurer.

Coverage limit

The maximum amount an insurance policy may pay under a specified coverage, subject to its terms.

Deductible

The amount you may need to pay toward a covered loss before certain benefits apply.

Exclusions

Situations, causes, or types of loss that a particular policy may not cover.
