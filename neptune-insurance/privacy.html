TRANSPARENCY / DEMO PRIVACY

How this demonstration handles data.

Website storage and voice demo information. Last updated November 23, 2026.

1. About this website

Neptune Insurance is a fictional portfolio and learning demonstration. It is not an insurance provider. It is maintained as part of an independent AWS customer experience architecture project.

2. Browser-only demo information

The claims wizard uses dropdown selections and a sample date. When you create a demo claim, the example details and a synthetic reference are stored in this browser's localStorage so the portal can display them. This prototype does not submit those records to a server. You can remove them through your browser's site-data settings.

3. Avoid real personal information

Do not type real names, addresses, policy identifiers, financial information, medical information, or actual accident details into the demo. Use synthetic examples only.

4. Third-party resources

Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts when online. Separate hosting and network services may process ordinary technical requests such as an IP address and browser metadata. The separate voice demo at 1-800-810-5374 uses Amazon Connect. Calling it creates contact records; flow logging is configured for lab diagnostics and can include menu selections and configured attributes. Use synthetic test information only. Browser-stored demo claims are not sent to the voice flow.

5. Proposed SMS program and consent

Cloud Systems Direct LLC develops and operates Neptune Insurance as a fictional demonstration project. A low-volume, non-marketing SMS notification program is planned solely for authorized test participants and simulated claims, service follow-ups, and account updates. No SMS enrollment or sending is currently active. The current demo number is for voice calling only; it has not been established as an approved SMS sender.

Once the system is ready, test participants will enter their own phone number and check a separate, unchecked SMS consent box on the SMS enrollment page. The page states the sender, use cases, approximate message frequency, potential message/data charges, and STOP/HELP instructions, along with links to this Privacy Policy and the Terms. Consent is optional. A secure backend must record the number, exact consent language, date/time, and enrollment source before any text is sent. The preview page does not collect information.

Participant mobile numbers and consent records will be used solely to administer authorized demonstration messaging and comply with opt-out and legal obligations. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messaging providers and carriers may process data for the limited purpose of delivering messages. Data access and retention must be appropriately restricted before activation.

After launch, message frequency will vary, normally 1–4 texts per test session. Message and data rates may apply. Recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Phone numbers will not be added to an SMS list merely because someone uses the website or calls the voice demonstration.
