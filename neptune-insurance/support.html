02 / ANSWERS, WITHOUT THE RUNAROUND

A few common questions.

Search our guidance or browse the topics below.

How can I report an auto accident? +

Use our guided demo claim form to practice first notice of loss. We don't accept real accidents or sensitive personal details through this prototype.

Where can I check a claim reference? +

Open My Neptune. Claims created in this demo are stored in this browser and displayed with fictional status information.

Can I ask to speak with a supervisor? +

Escalation to a supervisor is part of the target Amazon Connect routing architecture. It is not enabled on this static prototype.

How do I update my policy details? +

The portal shows sample policy data. Real policy changes would need identity verification and authorized backend insurance systems.

Can I get updates by SMS? +

SMS is planned, not active. In the target experience, customers opt in by voice with clear disclosures, and their consent must be captured before permitted texts are sent. A new toll-free number has not yet been assigned.

Does this website issue real quotes or coverage? +

No. Neptune Insurance is a fictional brand for a cloud architecture learning project. Nothing on this site sells coverage, binds insurance, or gives an actual premium.
