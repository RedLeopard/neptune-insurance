TRANSPARENCY / DEMO TERMS

Understand the experience.

Terms and limitations for this fictional insurance prototype. Last updated November 23, 2026.

1. Demonstration only

All brand descriptions, product categories, policies, rates, claim references, service levels, statuses, customer records and company representations on this site are simulated. Neptune Insurance is not a registered insurer or insurance agency.

2. No insurance transactions

This website does not quote, sell, bind, renew, cancel, modify, or administer actual insurance coverage. The quote explorer does not calculate true premiums and should not be used to make financial or insurance decisions.

3. No real claims or emergencies

Claims submitted in the guided demo remain in browser storage. They do not notify a claims adjuster or agency. For real emergencies, use appropriate local emergency services and contact your actual insurer directly.

4. Communication channels

Amazon Connect voice calling is configured at 1-800-810-5374 for lab testing; it does not provide real insurance service. Calling does not constitute SMS consent. SMS, email, and other channel integrations are planned and must be independently validated before use.

5. Proposed SMS program terms

Cloud Systems Direct LLC is preparing an optional Neptune Insurance Demo SMS program for authorized participants. After sender verification and activation, messages may include simulated claim updates, customer service confirmations, and demo account activity. These will be non-marketing messages based on fictional scenarios. SMS enrollment and delivery are not yet active.

When enrollment opens, participants must explicitly consent on the SMS program page. Message frequency varies, typically 1–4 messages per test session. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Opt-in is optional and is not a condition of using other demonstration features. For assistance, contact et@cloudsystemsdirect.com. See our Privacy Policy.

6. Data and security

Never enter real sensitive data. The demonstration does not implement authentication or a production insurance backend. Browser-only storage can be cleared in site settings.

7. Purpose

This website demonstrates frontend design and an AWS Amazon Connect omnichannel architecture for an insurance contact center training project created by Edward Thornton.
